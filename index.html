Burin Naowarat

Hello all! I’m a PhD student at the Centre for Speech Technology Research (CSTR) in the School of Informatics at the University of Edinburgh, supervised by Sharon Goldwater and Hao Tang.

I’m interested in how neural networks process information through the geometry of their representations, with the goals of understanding models, learning from them, and ultimately designing better ones. My current research focuses on identifying and examining emergent structures in deep speech models that correspond to phonetic content, speaker identity, and the timing of phones, syllables, and words.

Before starting my PhD, I completed a research master’s degree focused on automatic speech recognition (ASR) at Chulalongkorn University, supervised by Ekapol Chuangsuwanich. During my master’s, I interned with Amazon Alexa in Cambridge. I later worked as a language modeling engineer for on-device ASR systems at Cerence.

Feel free to get in touch! Email, GitHub, LinkedIn, Google Scholar
